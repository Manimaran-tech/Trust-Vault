TrustVault Secure repository of validated decisions and audit evidence.Problem StatementFinancial institutions rapidly adopt autonomous AI for customer service, fraud detection, approvals, and operations. While automation improves efficiency, it introduces risks like unauthorized actions, regulatory breaches, hallucinations, and reduced trust.Current AI automates decisions but lacks real-time governance. SentinelAI solves this by supervising autonomous financial agents, ensuring every AI decision is secure, policy-compliant, explainable, auditable, and monitored.SolutionTrustVault  is an enterprise Multi-Agent Governance Platform built on a Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture.Instead of relying on one LLM, specialized AI agents independently evaluate every request across:Identity VerificationFraud DetectionFinancial Risk AssessmentCompliance ValidationPolicy EnforcementExplainabilityAudit LoggingA Consensus Decision Engine approves actions only after expert agreement. A Governance Layer enforces permissions, spend limits, policy compliance, AI monitoring, and human approval when necessary.Why TrustVault ?Traditional AI asks:"Can AI complete this task?"TrustVault  asks:"Should AI be allowed to complete this task?"Every financial action is validated before execution, reducing:Unauthorized actionsAI hallucinationsPolicy violationsCompliance risksFinancial losseswhile improving security, transparency, and customer trust.InnovationTrustVault  introduces a Consensus-Based AI Governance Framework where multiple domain-specific expert agents independently evaluate requests before reaching a decision.Expert agents include:IdentityFraudRiskCompliancePolicyExplainabilityAn independent AI Watchdog monitors abnormal behavior, policy violations, confidence degradation, repeated disagreements, and model drift.System Architecture+---------------------------------+                    |
